I saw you toss the kites on high
**the kites on high, the kites on high**
and blow the birds about the sky
and all around I heard you pass
**I heard you pass, I heard you pass**
like layered skirts across the grass

_chorus:_
**O wind, O wind, a-blowing all day long
a-blowing, a-blowing, a-blowing all day long
O wind, O wind, that sings so loud a song,
that sings so loud a song!**

I saw the different things you did
**the things you did, the things you did**
but always you yourself you hid
I felt you push, I heard you call
**I heard you call, I heard you call**
I could not see yourself at all

O you that are so strong and cold
**so strong and cold, so strong and cold**
oh blower, are you young or old?
are you a beast of field or tree?
**of field or tree, of field or tree?**
or just a stronger child than me?

_lyrics adapted from The Wind, a poem by Robert Louis Stevenson_
