title: What the Winds Bring
composer: Emma Azelborn
voiceMemo: what the winds bring.m4a

Which is the Wind that brings the cold?
**The North-Wind! The North-Wind!**
and all the snow; and the sheep will scamper into the fold
**When the North begins to blow.**

Which is the Wind that brings the heat?
**The South-Wind! The South-Wind!**
and corn will grow, and peaches redden for you to eat,
**When the South begins to blow.**

Which is the Wind that brings the rain?
**The East-Wind! The East-Wind!**
and farmers know that cows come shivering up the lane
**When the East begins to blow.**

Which is the Wind that brings the flowers?
**The West-Wind! The West-Wind!**
and soft and low the birdies sing in the summer hours
**When the West begins to blow.**

_lyrics adapted from What the Winds Bring, a poem by Edmund Clarence Stedman_
