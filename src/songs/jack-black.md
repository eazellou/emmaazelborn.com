all rats heed what I say
for I have faced notorious **Jack Black**
a man with teeth, a fearless ratter
he's their queen's rat catcher

_chorus:_
hear his rat sash!
clatter clack clack!
rat ratatat!
stay back...
... scatter fast
stay back...
... scatter fast
Jack Black is on the attack

here, my tail, his ferrets bit it off
the teeth, the snarls commanded by **Jack Black**
I tore away, stump all bleeding
little bells rang while rats were screaming

_chorus_

my ribs could hardly draw a breath
in a pile of rats imprisoned by **Jack Black**
flung by the fist into iron-wire cages
stacking and squishing and slowly caving

_chorus_

I snapped, attacked, ferocious and fast
and bit to bone the thumb of **Jack Black**
tossed away, ran quick to cover
one more scar beside his others

_chorus_

go now and warn of his ways
the rat destroyer notorious **Jack Black**
poisoned pockets, he won't hurry
if he's caught you, death is mercy

_chorus_
