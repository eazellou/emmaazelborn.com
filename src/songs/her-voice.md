The ancient trees were falling, the sawmills ever grinding
Cathedral treetops burning by piles of ties and siding
When women called for action as the century began
Many hands and many voices defending redwood land

Carrie Stevens Walter and Josephine McCrackin
They saw untold destruction and wrote of what could happen
They called out "save the redwoods!" and spread it far and wide
And the women in their gardens took up the battle cry

Hundreds of club women joined Laura White in protest
For congress said they'd buy a grove but Joe refused to fund it
The women kept on pressing 'til he shouted "go to hell!"
Laura died never knowing if her giant sequoias fell

O'er thirty years these women followed Laura P Mahan
When loggers cut protected trees she sounded the alarm
She caught them in the act and threw her body in the way
So their axes never landed; those trees all stand today
